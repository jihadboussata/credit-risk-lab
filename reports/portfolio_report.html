The research decision

Which existing accounts should a bank review?

Six months of recorded bills, payments and repayment status are used to predict the source dataset's next-month default indicator. This is an educational prioritization study, not an approval system or a deployed banking product.

Validation-selected champion: XGBoost. Calibration: none. Policy threshold: 0.15, under an illustrative 5:1 missed-default:false-alarm cost ratio.

95% bootstrap intervals: average precision 0.551–0.607; ROC–AUC 0.778–0.807. Intervals condition on the fitted model and do not include selection uncertainty or future drift.

Selection discipline

18,000 train. 6,000 validate. 6,000 assess.

All model tuning uses three stratified folds within training. Validation selects the model family, optional probability calibration and policy. The test set is assessed after those decisions are frozen. Age, sex, customer ID and target are excluded from primary features.

Decision accounting

The consequence of the chosen threshold

The cost-selected policy is not capacity constrained: it flags 48.5% of test accounts. Under a separately prespecified top-10% ranking budget (600 accounts), precision is 72.3% and recall is 32.7%. This is a ranking benchmark with stable tie-breaking, not an estimated intervention benefit.

At the frozen policy, the test confusion counts are TN=2,816, FP=1,857, FN=276, TP=1,051. Precision is 36.1%; recall is 79.2%. The illustrative error cost is 539.5 units per 1,000 accounts, compared with 1105.8 for flagging none and 778.8 for flagging all. This comparison is a consequence of assumed classification costs. It is not measured money saved or evidence that reviewing an account prevents default.

Feature-engineering ablation: engineered minus original training-CV average precision +0.0070. This exploratory comparison holds hyperparameters fixed and does not independently tune each feature set.

Limits and next evidence

A historical prototype with explicit boundaries.

The source is Taiwan in 2005. Random holdout results do not establish performance in a future period or another bank. Missing direct protected attributes does not remove proxies. No intervention effects, measured financial savings, live monitoring or stakeholder approval are claimed.

Next evidence would require contemporary data, temporal and external validation, domain review and a prospective test of whether review actions help customers and reduce harm.
